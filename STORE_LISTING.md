# SonoBuddy AI — App Store & Google Play listing

Copy each field into App Store Connect / Play Console. Every character count below was checked
against the store's limit.

## Positioning

SonoBuddy AI is an **AI study companion for learning to read ultrasound images**. Search terms are
chosen so it doesn't compete with our other two apps:
- **SonoPass** owns registry exam prep: SPI, physics, mock exam, practice test, flashcards.
- **SonoBuddy** owns the scan-room reference: normal values, measurements, calculators.

SonoBuddy AI targets people *learning* ultrasound: POCUS, sonography students, anatomy,
eFAST/echo/OB/lung views, and "AI" plus "tutor" searches. The description cross-promotes the other two apps.

Every claim is educational (App Review guideline 1.4.1, which the earlier SonoPilot version was
rejected under): no "diagnose", "clinical", "detect" or "bedside", and nothing about patient care.

**Why these words.** Apple searches the name, subtitle and keywords together and combines words
across them, so no word is repeated:
- **Name and subtitle** cover *SonoBuddy, AI, ultrasound, study, learn, POCUS, sonography*.
- **Keywords** add who it's for (*student, sonographer, DMS, tutor*), what they study (*anatomy, echo,
  eFAST, FAST, OB/GYN, vascular, lung, cardiac, abdomen*) and what they do (*scan, image*).

Together they match searches like *ultrasound study app*, *POCUS learning*, *sonography student*,
*echo anatomy*, *eFAST tutor*, *learn ultrasound images*, *AI ultrasound* and *sonographer study*.

**Left out on purpose:**
- *Registry, SPI, physics, mock* belong to SonoPass.
- *Diagnose, clinical, detect, bedside* would contradict the educational positioning.
- *ARDMS* and credential names are trademarks; guideline 2.3.7 is a common rejection reason.

### Description (3282 / 4,000)

```
Learn to read ultrasound images with an AI study buddy that explains what's on the screen.

SonoBuddy AI is a study companion for sonography students, medical students, residents and anyone learning point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS). Snap a photo of an ultrasound screen or upload a saved image, and get a clear walkthrough: the main structures labeled, the key points explained, and study tips for what to look at next. Then ask follow-up questions about the same image.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Snap or upload an ultrasound image
2. Pick a protocol, or let the AI work out the view
3. Get a walkthrough with labeled structures, study points and reference measurements
4. Ask follow-up questions in plain language

STUDY ANY IMAGE
• Structures labeled on your image: liver, kidney, LV, aorta, gestational sac and more
• Study points that explain what's worth noticing and why it matters
• Reference measurements with typical values, explained for learners
• Study tips: the next view to get and how to improve the image
• Follow-up chat: "Why does fluid collect here first?" "What's the difference between A-lines and B-lines?"

34 PROTOCOLS, STEP BY STEP
eFAST, echo (PLAX, PSAX, apical 4-chamber, IVC), lung, OB/GYN and pelvis, abdomen (RUQ, gallbladder, renal, bladder, aorta), vascular (carotid, DVT, arterial and venous duplex), thyroid, shoulder, soft tissue, and neuro (optic nerve sheath, transcranial Doppler). Each protocol includes how to get every view, what to look for, a measurement guide with typical values, and common mistakes to avoid.

YOUR STUDY LIBRARY
• Every walkthrough is saved to your History so you can review it later
• Images stay on your device, shown alongside your notes
• Recommendations based on the areas you choose to study first

PRIVACY BUILT IN
• The top of each image, where machines usually show patient details, is blacked out before it's analyzed
• Images aren't stored on our servers; only your study notes sync to your account
• Delete your account and data anytime from the app

FREE AND PRO
Free accounts get 5 AI study sessions every month, with all 34 protocols.
SonoBuddy AI Pro unlocks unlimited study sessions and follow-up questions:
• Pro Monthly: $9.99 per month
• Pro Yearly: $69.99 per year
Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.
Terms of Use: https://www.sonobuddyai.com/terms
Privacy Policy: https://www.sonobuddyai.com/privacy

MADE FOR LEARNERS
Built for students in sonography (DMS) programs, medical students, residents and fellows, nurses, PAs and NPs adding ultrasound skills, and practicing sonographers cross-training into a new area.

Need a quick reference in the scan room? Try SonoBuddy, our ultrasound reference app. Studying for your registry exam? Try SonoPass, our exam prep app.

EDUCATIONAL USE ONLY
SonoBuddy AI is a study tool. It is not a medical device, is not FDA-cleared, and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Don't use it to make decisions about patient care. AI explanations can be wrong, and label positions are approximate.

Questions or feedback? Email support@sonobuddyai.com.
```

### App Review notes (App Review Information → Notes)

```
SonoBuddy AI is an educational study tool for students learning to read ultrasound images. It does not diagnose or give medical advice; every result is framed as study material.

How to test without an ultrasound image: Scan tab > "Try a demo analysis without uploading". This generates a sample walkthrough. You can also upload any ultrasound image from the web.

Subscriptions (auto-renewable, group "SonoBuddy AI Pro"): sonobuddyai_pro_monthly ($9.99/month) and sonobuddyai_pro_yearly ($69.99/year). Open them from the "Go Pro" card on Home, Scan or History. Restore Purchases, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links are on the same screen. Free accounts get 5 study sessions per month.

Account deletion: Home > menu (top right) > Account > "Delete account" at the bottom of the page.

Privacy: images are processed to generate the walkthrough and are not stored on our servers. A copy is kept only on the device (it can be turned off on the Account page).

Sign-in is required (email and password). Demo account details are in the Sign-in Information fields.
```

### Age rating questionnaire

- **Medical or treatment information:** answer **None**. It is educational anatomy content, not
  treatment advice. SonoPass answered the same way.

  walkthroughs and has an AI chat.
- Everything else: **None / No**.

### App Privacy ("nutrition label")

All data is **linked to the user** and **not used for tracking**:

| Data type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Contact Info → Email Address | App Functionality (account sign-in) |
| Contact Info → Name | App Functionality (greeting) |
| Identifiers → User ID | App Functionality (account) |
| User Content → Other User Content | App Functionality (saved study notes / scan history text) |
| Purchases → Purchase History | App Functionality (subscription status) |

**Photos: not collected.** Images are only processed to answer the request and aren't stored on our
servers. Apple's definition of "collect" excludes data that is processed in real time and not kept.
The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Collected:
- Email address, name and user IDs (account management)
- App activity → Other user-generated content (study notes)
- Photos: **collected, processed ephemerally, not stored**. They are sent to our AI provider only to
  create the walkthrough; that's a service provider, so it isn't "shared".

Other answers:
- Encrypted in transit: **Yes**.
- Users can request deletion: **Yes**. In the app: Account → Delete account. On the web:
  https://www.sonobuddyai.com/privacy#delete-account
- Shared with third parties: **No**.
