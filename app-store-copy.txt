DESCRIPTION  (4000 chars max — Apple shows ~250 chars before "More")
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SonoBuddy AI is an educational reference app that helps students and
learners study ultrasound imaging. Point your camera at any ultrasound
screen — AI labels anatomy, walks through reference measurements, and
highlights areas worth a closer look, purely for learning purposes.

SonoBuddy AI is an educational tool only. It does not provide medical
advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a substitute for
professional medical care. Always consult a licensed physician or
qualified healthcare provider for any medical questions or before
making any medical decisions.

── HOW IT WORKS ──
1. Snap a photo of any ultrasound screen (no special equipment needed)
2. AI walks through the image for learning purposes — labeling
   structures and showing reference measurements
3. Review the annotated walkthrough, or export a one-tap study summary

── 31+ PROTOCOLS COVERED (EDUCATIONAL REFERENCE) ──
• Emergency / eFAST (FAST, extended FAST)
• Cardiac Echo (parasternal, apical, subcostal views)
• OB/GYN (first trimester, biophysical profile)
• Vascular (DVT, aorta, IVC)
• Musculoskeletal (MSK)
• Lung / Pleural
• Renal & Bladder
• Soft Tissue
• And more

── KEY FEATURES ──
• AI-guided structure labeling for study purposes
• Reference measurement walkthroughs
• Educational highlights on areas worth further study
• AI chat — ask study questions about an image
• One-tap PDF study summary export
• Automatic patient data redaction (PHI protection)
• HIPAA-ready privacy architecture
• Works offline — view past study sessions without connectivity

── BUILT FOR LEARNERS ──
Designed as a study companion for medical students, residents, and
sonography students learning to recognize ultrasound anatomy and
point-of-care ultrasound (POCUS) concepts.

IMPORTANT: SonoBuddy AI is an educational tool only. It is NOT FDA-cleared,
does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and must never
be used as the basis for any clinical or medical decision. Always seek
the advice of a licensed physician or other qualified healthcare
provider with any questions regarding a medical condition.

── PLANS ──
Free: 5 AI study sessions per month, all 31 protocols
Pro: Unlimited study sessions — $9.99/month or $69.99/year
